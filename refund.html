Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Premium costs 100 ETB, paid once. Because the materials are digital and unlocked as soon as an admin approves your payment, payments are not refundable once access has been granted, except in the cases below.

We will refund (or fix) if

1. You paid but your payment was approved and the materials still do not open, and we cannot fix it within 7 days.
2. You were charged twice for the same account (we refund the extra payment).
3. You sent the payment to us by mistake and your payment has not been approved yet.

We will not refund if

You changed your mind after getting access, you did not like the content, you shared your account or the materials, or your account was closed for breaking the Terms.

If your payment is rejected

If we cannot verify a payment (for example the receipt number does not match), it is rejected and you will see a notice in the app. If you really did pay, email us with your receipt and we will check again.

How to ask

Email unomijr@gmail.com from your registered address with your receipt number and a short explanation, within 14 days of paying. Refunds are sent back by the same method (Telebirr or CBE) and usually take a few working days.

Approved refunds close the premium access on that account.

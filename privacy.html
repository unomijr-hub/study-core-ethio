Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This explains what personal data StudyCore keeps, why, and what you can ask us to do. In short: we keep only what the service needs, and we never sell your data.

1. What we collect

Account: your full name, email address, chosen stream and semester, and your password (stored only as a one-way hash, we cannot read it). Optional profile picture.

Payments: the payment method, the transaction or receipt number, and the screenshot you upload.

Study activity: course progress and quiz attempts and scores.

Technical data: your IP address and browser/device type, stored with your login session for security (for example to spot abuse). We also keep server logs.

2. Why we use it

To run your account, verify your email, check your payment and unlock materials, show your progress, protect the site from fraud and abuse, and contact you about your account. The email watermark on documents is used to trace leaked material.

3. Who handles it

We do not sell or rent your data. We use these services to run the site: Supabase (database and file storage, Frankfurt), Render (hosting, Frankfurt) and Brevo (sending emails). They process data only to provide their service to us. Admins of StudyCore can see your account details and payment proof.

4. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. Payment records and screenshots are kept as long as needed to deal with disputes and keep our records, then deleted. Login sessions and tokens expire automatically.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data, or to close your account, by emailing unomijr@gmail.com from your registered address. We will reply within a reasonable time. Some payment records may need to be kept after deletion where we have a genuine reason to (for example, a fraud or dispute check).

6. Security

Passwords are hashed, files are stored privately, and connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

7. Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Children

StudyCore is meant for university students. If you believe a child under 16 has registered, contact us and we will remove the account.

9. Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Contact: unomijr@gmail.com.
